About Me

A student who builds, leads,
and thinks big

I'm Muhamad Naufal Bin Muhamad Faizal, born 26 June 2008 in Hospital Pusrawi, Kuala Lumpur. I grew up in Shah Alam, Selangor and spent my secondary school years at MRSM ATM Bera, Pahang — where I graduated with First Class Honours on 17 December 2025.

My principal described me as "a pleasant person who is helpful and cooperative — with initiative and great determination to succeed." That's exactly how I try to show up every day.

Technology isn't just a subject to me. I started learning HTML at age 14, earned Python and Java certifications through Sololearn, and I'm now building an AI-powered chatbot to help students prepare for exams. My goal is to become a software / AI engineer who drives real change through intelligent systems.

Beyond academics, I completed the Khatam Al-Quran in May 2025 — a deeply meaningful personal achievement — and I've represented my school in chess at the national level multiple times.
