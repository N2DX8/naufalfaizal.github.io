About me

Who I am

I'm Muhamad Naufal Bin Muhamad Faizal — a recent SPM graduate from MRSM ATM Bera, Pahang, with a deep passion for software engineering, artificial intelligence, and creative technology.

My time at MRSM shaped how I think. I started out motivated by competition, but over the years I learned that real growth comes from purpose and self-improvement. That mindset shift led me to commit fully to one goal: becoming an AI engineer who builds things that matter.

I'm not just a consumer of technology — I build with it. I develop AI chatbots for education using practical no-code tools like Chatbase and Replit, edit cinematic videos in Premiere Pro and CapCut, and run my own YouTube channel, Naufal Academia. I also led the Chess Club, served as President of the Student Leaders Council, and represented my school in national-level competitions.

I'm currently pursuing scholarships and university opportunities so I can keep building, keep learning, and contribute to Malaysia's digital future.
